# NVIDIA Stock & Business Analysis

## Project Overview

This project analyzes NVIDIA’s stock-market performance, financial growth, competitive position, and business strategy in the artificial intelligence industry.

## Areas of Analysis

- Stock-price performance
- Revenue and profit growth
- AI-chip market trends
- Competitors such as AMD and Intel
- NVIDIA’s branding and marketing strategy
- Factors that may influence investor confidence

## Project Goal

The goal is to understand how NVIDIA’s financial performance, market position, and business strategy relate to its stock-market performance.

*This project is for educational purposes and is not financial advice.*
